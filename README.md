# repo-creative-thesis-

This repo collects creative thesis ideas for Frank's MSc AI thesis (Utrecht University, supervisor Dr. Ioana Karnstedt-Hulpuș). The ideas come from 8 studies from 2025–2026 that Frank collected. They were uploaded as PDFs on 7 Nov 2026; one of the 9 files is a duplicate.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `thesis_ideas.md` | **Start here.** A ranked table of all 8 ideas, the top 5 worked out, how they combine, and questions for the supervisor. |
| `study_summaries.md` | One section per study: summary, gaps the authors name, gaps I see, one thesis idea, and a feasibility score out of 35. |
| `NOTES.md` | Decision log: inventory, access problems, all kill-test search queries, and red flags found in the studies. |
| `references.bib` | Every cited work, with its verification level ([FT] / [ABS] / [UNVERIFIED]) in the `note` field. |

**The top 3 ideas at a glance**
1. I3: how good must a link predictor be before it helps a portfolio? (29/35)
2. I1: do the stock graphs that models learn match real company relations? (28/35)
3. I5: geopolitical tension as the switch for spillover edges between stock markets (28/35)
